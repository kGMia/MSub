# List editing and Japanese vocalizations — 2026-10-30

The cue-list TextField bindings, insert/delete actions, shift and gap-closing previously mutated FileSlot.cues without registering document undo. They now register slot-ID-scoped snapshots containing cues, selected cue and table selection. Restore registers its inverse for redo and refreshes serialized preview. Text/speaker typing coalesces within a two-second same-file/cue/field burst; selection/file/tab changes, submit, undo/redo and list structural operations break the burst. History is capped at80 levels. A no-op value change does not register history. Timing calibration and translation retain their existing undo paths.

Bulk insert/delete/shift/gap changes now assemble a local cue array and publish once, reducing repeated observable mutations. Reason badges are Equatable by text/language so playback/selection changes need not reclassify unchanged text. Markup normalization reuses a compiled regex and skips it when there is no markup; Japanese matching indexes candidate units by initial character.

Japanese whole-cue vocal units now include repeated うん and elongated/exclamatory わー/わあ/わぁ/わっ and うわ variants. Katakana/half-width forms continue to normalize. Ordinary sentences such as うん、わかった and わー、きれい, and words うんどう/うわさ remain unflagged. This is text-based candidate classification; it does not delete cues automatically or infer intent acoustically.
